Privacy Policy

Arrowmora: Escape Puzzle · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Arrowmora is an offline puzzle game. We do not run servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

Data that stays on your device

Your progress, coins, settings and achievements are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot see or recover them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers other than the advertising ID (Arrowmora does not ask for tracking permission, so the IDFA is not shared);
  • your approximate location, inferred from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and tap;
  • crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off interstitial ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple which items you own.

Children

Arrowmora is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Reklamlar

Ücretsiz sürüm Google AdMob reklamları gösterir. Reklamları göstermek ve ölçmek için Google şunları toplayabilir: reklam kimliği dışındaki cihaz tanımlayıcıları (uygulama izleme izni istemez, bu yüzden IDFA paylaşılmaz), IP adresinden çıkarılan yaklaşık konum, gördüğün ve dokunduğun reklamlarla ilgili bilgiler, reklam SDK'sının çökme ve performans verileri. Google bu verileri kendi politikasına göre işler: policies.google.com/privacy.
